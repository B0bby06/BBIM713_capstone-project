01 / The project

From monitoring to foresight.

Air-quality measurements reveal what has happened. Forecasts can help environmental decision-makers prepare for what may happen next.

◉

The business challenge

Traffic, air-quality index and pollutant observations were stored in different formats and time intervals, making consistent analysis difficult.

↗

Our objective

Build a minimum viable product (MVP) to analyse data and forecast PM2.5 and NO₂ concentrations up to 24 hours ahead.

▤

Intended value

Turn complex model outputs into more understandable trends, evaluation evidence and decision-support visuals for stakeholders.

Project scope: Queen Street / Auckland City Centre. This is a student-built decision-support prototype in an Auckland Council stakeholder context—not a deployed or official Auckland Council forecasting service.

Important interpretation: Extra Trees was selected using validation results for both pollutants. Histogram Gradient Boosting achieved a lower NO₂ holdout RMSE in the report, but holdout results should not be used to re-select a model after evaluation.

What the EDA revealed

NO₂ and NO were strongly positively correlated in the study (r ≈ 0.807). The relationship between PM2.5 and PM10 was also positive (r ≈ 0.767). Traffic volume showed a stronger association with NO₂ than with PM2.5 in this dataset.

Source: team report and accompanying project presentation. Values are historical evaluation findings; these are not live predictions or public-health warnings.

06 / Responsible analytics

Limitations matter.

The aim is evidence-based decision support, not overstated certainty. Data permissions, confidentiality and data-governance obligations must be checked before public sharing.

Data privacy and permissions

The project aimed not to intentionally collect identifying personal data. Dataset ownership, permitted use and any release restrictions still require verification before publishing raw data.

Quality and uncertainty

Some observations were missing or uneven. Ozone (O₃) was excluded because suitable data were not available. Backtesting accuracy varied across periods.

Honest interpretation

Forecasts are estimates, not measured facts. A production system would require future weather and traffic information, dashboard fixes, monitoring and ongoing evaluation.
